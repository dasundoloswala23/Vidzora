Vidzora Privacy Policy

Save. Watch. Share.

Effective date: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Vidzora ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. Vidzora lets you paste a link from a supported social media platform and save media you are authorized to download.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Vidzora does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the App's core features.

2. Information We Process

  • Pasted links: When you paste a video/media link and tap "Get Media", that link is sent to a third-party media-lookup service to retrieve the downloadable media (thumbnail, title, and file links). We do not store the links you paste on our servers.
  • Downloaded files and history: Information about files you download (title, platform, file size, date) is stored only locally on your device, so it never leaves your phone unless you explicitly share a file yourself.
  • App settings: Preferences such as download quality, theme, and toggles are stored locally on your device.
  • Device storage / gallery access: The App requests storage/photo permissions solely to save downloaded media to your device's gallery.

3. Advertising

Vidzora shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads to unlock certain downloads) using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use technical data - such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and app usage - to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. This processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and, where applicable, the Google AdMob data disclosure. You can manage ad personalization through your device's ad settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Vidzora uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Firestore) - used to remotely configure the App's media-lookup service without requiring an app update.
  • Google AdMob - used to display ads, as described above.
  • A third-party media-lookup API - used to resolve the links you paste into downloadable media. The link you paste is sent to this provider solely to perform the lookup.

5. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for only downloading content you own or are otherwise authorized to save, and for complying with the terms of service of the platform the content came from and applicable copyright law. Vidzora does not host, own, or control any third-party content.

6. Data Retention

Locally stored data (settings and download history) remains on your device until you clear it from the App's Settings screen or uninstall the App. We do not maintain a server-side account or profile tied to your identity.

7. Children's Privacy

Vidzora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by the App. No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
